## [](#p-1029-what-you-will-learn-1)What You Will Learn

Unlock the power of visual personal knowledge management right inside Obsidian! The **Excalidraw Essentials Mini Course** is carefully designed to help you hit the ground running with the Obsidian Excalidraw plugin.

Over the course of 10 highly practical lessons, you will discover the core features and advanced use-cases that make Excalidraw such a versatile tool. From creating your first drawing and managing custom templates, to embedding interactive PDFs, extending functionality with custom scripts, and mastering the unique “postcard” concept (where markdown text meets infinite canvas)—this series gives you the foundational skills needed to supercharge your visual workflow.
