# Daily Nutrient Guide for an Active 40-Year-Old

**Baseline assumptions:** male, ~75 kg (165 lb), moderately-to-vigorously active (training 4–6x/week, mix of cardio + resistance). Where female needs differ meaningfully, it's noted in the table. Adjust up/down ~10-15% for body weight or activity level outside this range.

**How to read the columns:**
- **Min** – the floor below which deficiency risk becomes real over time
- **Recommended** – the target for someone active at this age (often above the generic RDA, since training increases turnover/losses)
- **Max (UL)** – Tolerable Upper Intake Level; a safety ceiling, not a goal

## 4. How Much Food That Actually Takes

Rough portions to hit the **recommended** target for key nutrients (not everything at once — these are per-nutrient references you mix and match across the day).

| Nutrient | Food Source | Amount Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Protein (120–150g) | Chicken breast, eggs, Greek yogurt, fish, legumes | ~200g chicken breast (60g) + 3 eggs (18g) + 200g Greek yogurt (20g) + 150g lentils (12g) + a protein shake (25g) ≈ 135g |
| Fiber (30-38g) | Oats, beans, vegetables, berries, whole grains | 1 cup oats (8g) + 1 cup black beans (15g) + 2 cups mixed veg (6g) + 1 cup raspberries (8g) |
| Calcium (1,000mg) | Dairy, fortified plant milk, sardines, leafy greens | 2 cups milk/yogurt (600mg) + 100g firm tofu (200mg) + 1 cup cooked kale (180mg) |

## 5. Food vs. Supplements — Where to Get What

**Get these from food whenever possible** (well-absorbed, hard to overdose, comes with synergistic nutrients like fiber and phytochemicals):
- Protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber
- Vitamin C, potassium, most B vitamins
- Calcium (dairy/plant sources are efficiently absorbed)
- Zinc, copper, manganese

**Reasonable to supplement** (many people fall short even with a good diet, or absorption/practicality favors a pill):
- **Vitamin D** – sun exposure and food sources rarely provide enough, especially at higher latitudes or with mostly-indoor lifestyles. Testing blood levels (25-OH D) is worthwhile before dosing.
- **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)** – unless you eat fatty fish 2-3x/week, a supplement closes the gap more reliably than plant ALA sources (poor conversion rate to EPA/DHA).
- **Magnesium** – common shortfall due to soil depletion and processed diets; forms like glycinate or citrate are well tolerated.
- **B12** – essential supplement territory for vegans/vegetarians, since it's only reliably found in animal products.
- **Iron** – only supplement if a blood test confirms low ferritin; unnecessary iron supplementation in men can cause harm (oxidative stress, GI upset) since there's no good way for the body to excrete excess.
- **Creatine** (not in the tables above since it isn't an essential nutrient, but relevant for an active 40-year-old) – 3-5g/day is one of the most evidence-backed additions for strength, muscle retention, and possibly cognitive benefits with aging.

**General rule:** whole food should be the default because it delivers nutrients bundled with fiber, water, and hundreds of other compounds that supplements strip away. Supplements make the most sense when (a) a blood test shows a genuine deficiency, (b) the nutrient is very hard to get from food given your diet pattern, or (c) it's genuinely difficult to eat enough volume of food to reach the target (magnesium and omega-3 are the classic examples).

## 6. Two-Week Meal Plan

Targets ~2,700-2,900 kcal/day, ~140g protein, and covers the micronutrients above through variety. Adjust portion sizes up/down for your specific calorie needs. "Snack" slots can be swapped freely.

### Week 1

**Day 1**
- Breakfast: 3-egg omelet with spinach + feta, 1 slice whole grain toast, 1 orange
- Lunch: Grilled chicken breast (180g), quinoa (1 cup cooked), roasted broccoli + bell pepper, olive oil
- Snack: Greek yogurt (200g) with walnuts and berries
- Dinner: Baked salmon (150g), roasted sweet potato, sautéed kale with garlic

**Day 2**
- Breakfast: Overnight oats (1 cup oats, milk, chia seeds, banana, almond butter)
- Lunch: Turkey and black bean chili (with kidney beans, tomatoes, peppers), side salad
- Snack: Apple + 30g almonds
- Dinner: Lean beef stir-fry (150g) with broccoli, snap peas, brown rice

**Day 3**
- Breakfast: Greek yogurt parfait with granola, mixed berries, pumpkin seeds
- Lunch: Tuna salad (canned tuna, olive oil, mixed greens, chickpeas, cherry tomatoes)
- Snack: Cottage cheese with pineapple
- Dinner: Grilled chicken thighs, roasted potatoes, steamed green beans

**Day 4**
- Breakfast: Whole grain toast, 2 eggs, avocado, orange slices
- Lunch: Lentil soup with whole grain roll, side of carrots and hummus
- Snack: Protein shake + banana
- Dinner: Baked cod (150g), farro, roasted brussels sprouts

**Day 5**
- Breakfast: Smoothie (spinach, banana, protein powder, milk, flaxseed)
- Lunch: Grilled chicken Caesar-style salad (light dressing), whole grain roll
- Snack: Trail mix (almonds, pumpkin seeds, dried apricots)
- Dinner: Beef and vegetable stir-fry with brown rice

**Day 6**
- Breakfast: Veggie omelet (mushrooms, peppers, onion), side of berries
- Lunch: Turkey sandwich on whole grain bread with avocado, side salad
- Snack: Greek yogurt with honey and walnuts
- Dinner: Grilled salmon, quinoa, roasted asparagus

**Day 7**
- Breakfast: Protein pancakes (oats, eggs, banana, protein powder), berries
- Lunch: Chickpea and vegetable grain bowl with tahini dressing
- Snack: Cottage cheese + sliced peach
- Dinner: Roast chicken, mashed sweet potato, sautéed spinach

### Week 2

**Day 8**
- Breakfast: 3-egg scramble with tomatoes and spinach, toast
- Lunch: Grilled salmon salad (mixed greens, chickpeas, olive oil dressing)
- Snack: Banana + almond butter
- Dinner: Turkey meatballs, whole wheat pasta, side salad

**Day 9**
- Breakfast: Overnight oats with berries and chia seeds
- Lunch: Black bean and quinoa bowl with roasted vegetables, avocado
- Snack: Greek yogurt with granola
- Dinner: Grilled steak (150g), roasted potatoes, green beans

**Day 10**
- Breakfast: Greek yogurt with berries, pumpkin seeds, honey
- Lunch: Grilled chicken wrap (whole wheat tortilla, veggies, hummus)
- Snack: Apple + string cheese
- Dinner: Baked cod, farro, roasted broccoli

**Day 11**
- Breakfast: Veggie and egg breakfast burrito (whole wheat tortilla)
- Lunch: Lentil salad with feta, cucumber, tomato, olive oil
- Snack: Protein shake + handful of walnuts
- Dinner: Shrimp stir-fry with vegetables and brown rice

**Day 12**
- Breakfast: Smoothie (mango, spinach, Greek yogurt, protein powder)
- Lunch: Turkey chili with kidney beans, side salad
- Snack: Cottage cheese with pineapple
- Dinner: Grilled chicken breast, sweet potato mash, sautéed kale

**Day 13**
- Breakfast: Whole grain toast, avocado, poached eggs, orange
- Lunch: Tuna and white bean salad, whole grain crackers
- Snack: Trail mix
- Dinner: Baked salmon, quinoa, roasted brussels sprouts

**Day 14**
- Breakfast: Protein oats with banana and peanut butter
- Lunch: Chicken and vegetable grain bowl with tahini dressing
- Snack: Greek yogurt with berries
- Dinner: Roast turkey breast, mashed potatoes, green beans

**Standing recommendations across all 14 days:**
- 3-4 L water daily, more on training days
- Coffee/tea fine within reason; count toward fluids
- Rotate fish choices (salmon, cod, tuna, shrimp) to spread omega-3 and iodine intake
- If following a vegetarian/vegan version: swap animal proteins for tofu, tempeh, seitan, legumes, and add a B12 + algae-oil omega-3 supplement, since these are the two nutrients hardest to hit from a plant-only version of this plan
