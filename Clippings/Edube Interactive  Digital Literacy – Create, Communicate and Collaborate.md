## Create, Communicate and Collaborate

This course and its milestone exam focus on developing and validating practical digital communication and collaboration skills. You'll learn to:

- Create, organize, and share professional digital documents
- Communicate effectively using email, messaging, and video conferencing tools
- Collaborate efficiently in hybrid and remote work environments
- Apply digital well-being and sustainable technology practices

Each course in the Digital Literacy learning collection builds essential skills through videos, step-by-step tutorials using real software, interactive exercises, problem-solving activities, and environmental tips—alongside more traditional learning materials and quizzes.

### You will learn how to:

- Create and format digital documents for clear, professional communication.
- Communicate appropriately through email, instant messaging, and video calls.
- Collaborate effectively across teams and platforms using digital tools.
- Apply digital well-being and sustainability principles to support healthy, responsible technology use.

### Certification

**DL-00-C0x** milestone exam details:

- 28 questions, including single-select, multi-select, and dropdown or matching questions
- 40-minute time limit
- Passing score: 65%
